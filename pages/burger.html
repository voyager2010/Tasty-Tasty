Information about burger

A burger is a popular sandwich consisting of a cooked, often grilled, ground meat patty (typically beef) served inside a sliced bun. Originating in the late 19th/early 20th century in the US/Germany, it is commonly topped with cheese, lettuce, tomato, onions, and condiments like ketchup or mayonnaise

  1. Step 1: Prep & Shape: Mix 500g ground beef with salt, pepper, and desired seasonings (like worcestershire sauce or garlic powder). Shape into 4-6 patties, making them slightly wider than the buns and creating a shallow dimple in the center to prevent bulging.
  2. Step 2: Cook: Heat a pan or grill over medium-high heat. Cook the patties for 3-4 minutes per side, adding cheese in the last minute to melt, until they reach an internal temperature of 160°F (70°C)
  3. Step 3: Assemble: Toast the buns, spread with sauce, and layer the patty, lettuce, tomato, and onion.
