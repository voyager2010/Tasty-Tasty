Information about Biryani

Biryani is a celebrated, aromatic Indian rice dish made by layering fragrant basmati rice, marinated meat (or vegetables), and spices, often slow-cooked together using the dum (steaming) method. Originating from Persian roots (meaning "fried before cooking"), it was likely brought to India by travellers and perfected in royal Mughal kitchens.

  1. Marinate & Cook Chicken: Marinate chicken with yogurt, spices (garam masala, turmeric, chili powder), ginger-garlic paste, and lemon juice for 30 minutes to 24 hours. Cook this in a pot with oil and fried onions until the chicken is tender and oil separates.
  2. Prepare Rice: Rinse basmati rice until water is clear, then soak for 30 minutes. Boil in water with whole spices (cardamom, cloves) until it is about 70-80% cooked (slightly firm). Drain the rice.
  3. Layer and Dum: Layer the cooked chicken gravy and rice in a heavy-bottomed pot, starting with chicken, followed by rice. Top with fried onions, mint, coriander, and ghee. Cover tightly (using foil or dough) and cook on low heat for 15-20 minutes to combine flavors.
