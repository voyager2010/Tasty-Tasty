Information about pizza

Pizza is a globally popular Italian dish originating from Naples, featuring a leavened wheat-based dough topped with ingredients like tomatoes, mozzarella, and oil, traditionally baked in a wood-fired oven. Modern pizza often includes diverse toppings, with pepperoni as a top favorite, and is popular in various styles worldwide, such as Neapolitan, New York, and Chicago-style.

  1. Step 1: Prepare Dough & Oven - Preheat oven to the highest temperature (450-500°F/230-260°C) with a baking sheet inside. Roll out 1 lb of dough to a 12-inch circle.
  2. Step 2: Add Toppings - Spread cup pizza sauce, leaving a inch border. Top with shredded mozzarella and fresh basil leaves.
  3. Step 3: Bake - Transfer to the hot baking sheet and bake for 10-15 minutes until the crust is golden and cheese is bubbly
